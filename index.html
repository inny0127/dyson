How this was made

The model, the Blender scenes and the research behind them are in the project repository: docs/architecture.md (why sails and pods), docs/future-tech.md (what hardware actually flies), docs/earth.md (what Earth allows), docs/data/ (every study's numbers and sources) and src/model/hybrid.js (the model, with tests). Photographs are path traced in Blender Cycles at the true solar irradiance for the orbit; thermal images map 8–14 µm band radiance to a fixed false-colour scale.
